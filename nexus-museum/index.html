EAST AEROSPACE WING • IPRC MAHENDRAGIRI

ISRO CE-20 Cryogenic Rocket Engine

India's most powerful cryogenic rocket engine, designed and test-fired at the ISRO Propulsion Complex (IPRC) Mahendragiri in Tirunelveli district. Powers the LVM3 upper stage for Chandrayaan-3 and Gaganyaan.

Thrust (Vacuum) 200 kN (20 Tons)
Specific Impulse 443 Seconds
Propellants LOX (-183°C) / LH2 (-253°C)
Origin Facility IPRC Mahendragiri, TN
