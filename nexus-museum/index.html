CE-20 Cryogenic Rocket Engine

The CE-20 is India's most powerful cryogenic rocket engine, developed at ISRO Propulsion Complex (IPRC) Mahendragiri, Tirunelveli district. Powered by liquid oxygen (-183°C) and liquid hydrogen (-253°C).

Thrust (Vacuum) 200 kN (20 tons)
Specific Impulse 443 seconds
Facility Origin IPRC Mahendragiri, TN
Propellant Mix LOX / LH2 Gas Generator
