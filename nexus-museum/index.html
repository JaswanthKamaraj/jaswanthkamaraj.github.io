ISRO CE-20 Cryogenic Rocket Engine

The CE-20 is India's most powerful cryogenic rocket engine, designed and test-fired at ISRO Propulsion Complex (IPRC) Mahendragiri in Tirunelveli district. Powers the upper stage of LVM3.

Thrust (Vacuum) 200 kN (20 Tons)
Specific Impulse 443 Seconds
Testing Complex IPRC Mahendragiri, TN
Launch Missions Chandrayaan-3, Gaganyaan
