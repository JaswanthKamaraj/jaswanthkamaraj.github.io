ISRO CE-20 Cryogenic Rocket Engine

The CE-20 cryogenic rocket engine was designed and validated at the ISRO Propulsion Complex (IPRC) Mahendragiri in Tirunelveli district. It powers the upper stage of the LVM3 launch vehicle.

Propellant LOX / LH2 (-253°C)
Thrust 200 kN (20 Tons)
Testing Complex IPRC Mahendragiri, TN
Launch Missions Chandrayaan-3, Gaganyaan
